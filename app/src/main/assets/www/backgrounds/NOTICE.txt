Underwater Diving and Forest of Illusion
Original pixel artwork: Luis Zuno (Ansimuz)
https://opengameart.org/content/underwater-diving-pack
https://opengameart.org/content/sunnyland-forest-of-illusion
Both artwork packs are listed as CC0 on their author-uploaded OpenGameArt pages.
Original archive license notices are reproduced below.

Adaptation for Wayfinder: background layers composited, cropped to 16:9,
and scrolled at different speeds into seamless 48-second animated WebP loops.
768 x 432, 12.5 fps, lossless WebP, infinite repeat, no audio.
No bundled music, code, characters, or enemies from either pack are included.
